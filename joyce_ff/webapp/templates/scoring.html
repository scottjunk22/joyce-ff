🏈 Point System

Steve Joyce Fantasy Football
Scoring is by threshold, not per yard. You get nothing until you reach a line, then the full amount. A 74-yard rushing game scores 0; 75 yards scores 2. There is no partial credit and nothing is ever negative.
You draft 11 and start 9 each week: Coach, Kicker, DEF/ST, QB, 2 RB and 3 R (receivers — WR and TE together). Coach, Kicker, DEF/ST and QB are whole NFL team units, so every quarterback that team plays scores into your QB slot.
